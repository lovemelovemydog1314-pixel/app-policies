隐私摘要

应用主要在你的 Mac 上运行。除非你主动使用邮件、系统功能或网络共享功能，应用不会把文件内容发送给开发者。

应用处理的信息

创建、浏览、解压和校验都在本机进行。应用仅在 macOS 授权范围内访问你选择的文件。

本机保存与权限

归档密码只用于当前任务，不会上传或共享。SHA-256 匹配表示内容与清单值一致，不验证发送者身份。

应用不提供开发者运营的云同步或广告跟踪服务。系统功能及第三方服务仅在你主动使用时参与。

保留、删除与联系

ZipMaster Privacy Policy & Disclaimer

Thank you for using ZipMaster (hereinafter referred to as "the App"). We attach great importance to your privacy and data security. This page explains how the App collects, uses, and protects your information.

I. Privacy Policy

1. Information Collection

The App does not actively collect any personal identifiable information . During use, the App will never require you to register, log in, upload, or synchronize any personal data. The App may access your local file system solely to perform file compression, extraction, or browsing. These operations are executed exclusively on your local device and will never be uploaded to any remote servers.

2. File and Data Processing

All compression and extraction operations are executed locally on your device;

The App does not access, transmit, or store your file contents;

Temporary files required for tasks are used strictly during processing and are automatically purged upon completion.

3. Logs and Diagnostic Information

Unless you proactively choose to "Send Feedback" or "Report an Issue", the App does not collect any diagnostic logs or error reports. If you reach out to us, we may collect minimal necessary information (such as macOS version, App build number) to assist in resolving the issue.

4. Third-Party Services

The App does not embed any third-party analytics, advertising, tracking, or cloud storage SDKs. All functionalities are implemented locally on your device.

5. Data Security

The App strictly complies with the macOS App Sandbox security model, accessing files only within the scope authorized by the user. For encrypted archives, password information is used only for the current task and is never uploaded or shared.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 years of age and does not collect any information from children.

7. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will supersede earlier versions and be published within the App.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us via:

Email: zipmaster_support@126.com

II. Disclaimer

1. Scope of Use

The App is intended solely for lawful file compression and extraction in personal or commercial environments. Users must not use the App for any unlawful activities.

2. Data Risks

Please ensure you have backed up important data before processing files. The developer assumes no responsibility for file damage or loss caused by hardware malfunction, operating system errors, or user mistakes.

3. Compatibility and Performance

The App has been tested on officially supported macOS versions. However, compatibility issues may still arise on specific configurations or rare file formats. We assume no liability for performance fluctuations or third-party format parsing errors.

4. Third-Party Format Support

The App supports various common compression and archive formats and may utilize open-source components. These components are governed by their respective licenses, and the developer assumes no legal liability for their internal implementations.

5. Password Protection Feature

When encrypting archives with a password, please keep your password safe. If lost, the App cannot recover encrypted files and is not responsible for any resulting data loss.

6. Software Updates

We may release updates periodically to enhance performance or resolve issues. Users are responsible for keeping the App updated to ensure optimal experience and security.

III. Additional Notice

ZipMaster is an independently developed file utility with no affiliation or partnership with Apple Inc. or other third-party software vendors. All trademarks and format names (ZIP, 7z, RAR, etc.) are the property of their respective owners and are used strictly for descriptive compatibility purposes.
