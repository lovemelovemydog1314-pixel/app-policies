ZipMaster Privacy Policy & Disclaimer

Effective Date: March 1, 2026

Thank you for using ZipMaster (hereinafter referred to as "the App"). We attach great importance to your privacy and data security. This page explains how the App collects, uses, and protects your information.

I. Privacy Policy

1. Information Collection

The App does not actively collect any personal identifiable information. During use, the App will never require you to register, log in, upload, or synchronize any personal data. The App may access your local file system solely to perform file compression, extraction, or browsing. These operations are executed exclusively on your local device and will never be uploaded to any remote servers.

2. File and Data Processing

  • All compression and extraction operations are executed locally on your device;
  • The App does not access, transmit, or store your file contents;
  • Temporary files required for tasks are used strictly during processing and are automatically purged upon completion.

3. Logs and Diagnostic Information

Unless you proactively choose to "Send Feedback" or "Report an Issue", the App does not collect any diagnostic logs or error reports. If you reach out to us, we may collect minimal necessary information (such as macOS version, App build number) to assist in resolving the issue.

4. Third-Party Services

The App does not embed any third-party analytics, advertising, tracking, or cloud storage SDKs. All functionalities are implemented locally on your device.

5. Data Security

The App strictly complies with the macOS App Sandbox security model, accessing files only within the scope authorized by the user. For encrypted archives, password information is used only for the current task and is never uploaded or shared.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 years of age and does not collect any information from children.

7. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will supersede earlier versions and be published within the App.
