隐私政策

生效日期：2026-10-23

1. 适用范围与联系方式

本政策适用于 iPhone 版 Couple（小家）及其小组件，由 Couple 维护者发布。我们不提供独立于 Apple 账户的注册系统，不运营用于收集应用资料的自建服务器，也未接入广告、跨应用追踪或第三方统计 SDK。隐私与支持问题请联系 zipmaster_support@126.com。

应用数据会按你使用的功能保存在本机、Apple CloudKit 与 iCloud 云盘。公开政策和支持网页由 GitHub Pages 托管；访问网页时，GitHub 会按其自身政策处理网页请求。

2. 处理的数据与用途

你主动填写的资产余额、账本收支、预算、结算、目标、愿望、纪念日、待办、心情与留言、互动记录，用于展示、统计、提醒、共同管理和导出。头像与称呼用于识别小家成员。

证件资料可能包含姓名、号码后四位、有效期、存放位置、备注、扫描件、PDF 和 OCR 文本。家庭资料可能包含保险、医疗信息、血型、过敏、用药、紧急联系人姓名、关系和电话。请只添加你有权保存和分享的信息。

开启位置共享后，应用处理坐标、定位精度和更新时间，让共享小家成员查看你的最近位置。位置共享默认关闭，可随时关闭；关闭时会尝试删除该成员云端的最近位置，网络失败时应重试。

个人 iCloud 自动备份默认开启，可在“我们 → iCloud 数据持久化”关闭。备份包含上述业务资料、备份时间、摘要和设备名称，不包含位置共享记录和个人头像。恢复当前共享会话所需的信息保存在本机，并尝试保存到该 Apple 账户的私有 CloudKit 和 iCloud 云盘。小组件仅使用本机 App Group 快照，不直接连接 CloudKit。

3. iCloud 与共同小家

个人备份保存在当前 Apple 账户的 CloudKit 私有空间和 iCloud 云盘。创建的小家通过 CloudKit 共享空间同步业务资料、附件、头像和可选位置；共享成员可以读取和修改这些共同内容。

创建者在绑定页分享小家二维码图片，对方扫描后通过 Apple 的 CloudKit 共享完成绑定。二维码内包含一次性共享授权信息，绑定后会关联领取者的 Apple 账户；请只把二维码图片交给预期成员。新版本不会将该授权信息写入 CloudKit 公共数据库；恢复当前共享会话所需的信息保存在本机，并尝试保存到该 Apple 账户的私有 CloudKit 和 iCloud 云盘。

历史版本的小家可能保留 CloudKit 公共数据库中的共享快照、成员记录和绑定映射。创建者升级后会尝试迁移到新的共享方式并清理旧映射；数据删除也会尝试清理遗留记录，遇到权限不足会提示未完成。

4. 系统权限

相机用于扫描证件和小家二维码；OCR 与二维码识别均在设备上完成。系统照片选择器只向应用提供你选定的图片；识别二维码时，应用不会访问相册中的其他图片，也不会保存所选二维码图片。头像图片会按资料功能保存在本机，并可能同步至小家或个人备份。

Face ID 或设备密码由系统验证，应用不会取得面容模板或设备密码。通知用于纪念日、证件、待办、预算和对方动态提醒。定位权限仅在主动开启位置共享后请求；后台定位用于 App 不在前台时更新最近位置，实际更新受 iOS、电量与网络限制，不能保证实时或用作紧急救援。

可以在 App 和 iOS 设置中管理这些权限。拒绝可选权限不会阻止使用无关的本地记录功能。

5. 数据保护与分享边界

Apple 根据其服务与安全机制保护 CloudKit 和 iCloud 中的数据。应用不对所有资料作“端到端加密”或“任何第三方绝不可能访问”的承诺。共享资料会向有权限的小家成员开放。

单独填写的完整证件号码在本机使用 AES-GCM 加密，密钥存放于当前设备钥匙串且不随应用备份同步；加密后的字段可能随资料同步或备份。扫描件、PDF、OCR 文本或备注仍可能包含完整号码及其他敏感内容，并可被共享成员读取。请在上传或分享前检查。

你主动导出的 PDF、CSV、截图、系统剪贴板内容和分享给其他 App 的文件，由你和接收方另行管理。

6. 保存、删除与退出

入口为“我们 → 隐私与安全 → 删除数据”。确认前会说明当前操作影响的成员和数据范围。普通“解除本机绑定”只停止本机连接，不删除共享小家。

创建者：删除当前连接的小家及其 CloudKit 共享内容、附件、头像、历史绑定映射和可访问的旧版记录。对方将失去该小家的共享访问；删除不会替你清理对方另行保存的本地副本或个人备份。

加入者：删除共享空间中的本人称呼与头像、最近位置、心情、心跳、归属本人的证件及附件、相关证件任务与编辑记录、归属本人的待办和宠物互动时间，然后退出共享。共同资产、账本、愿望、纪念日、旅行说明及无法按成员区分的家庭/健康资料仍归创建者保留；其中如有你的个人信息，请在退出前逐项删除。

上述操作还删除当前 Apple 账户的 Couple 个人 CloudKit 备份、iCloud 云盘备份与绑定恢复文件，以及当前设备上的业务资料、头像缓存、文档加密密钥、小组件快照、提醒和应用临时导出文件。未绑定时只处理本机和个人备份；以前已解绑的小家需重新连接后单独处理。

删除需要当前 Apple 账户、网络及 iCloud 云盘可用。云端步骤失败时不显示全部完成；应用保留删除进度并暂停同步、备份与自动恢复，直到你重试完成。完成后自动备份保持关闭，需要主动重新开启。删除不注销 Apple 账户。

未连接小家时，也可明确选择“仅清除本机（保留云端）”，无需登录 iCloud。此选择不删除个人云端备份或以前的小家；会暂停自动恢复与备份，直到你主动重新启用相关功能。

7. 保留的副本、权利与政策更新

记录通常保留到你在 App 中删除相应项目或执行上述数据删除。单项删除共享项目不会自动清除已有的个人历史备份。对方设备、本账户其他设备、已导出文件、系统设备备份和 Apple 依法保留的服务记录不一定会随当前设备操作一并消失；请分别管理这些副本，避免从旧备份重新导入已删除资料。

我们不出售你的数据，也不用于广告画像。可通过 App 管理权限、导出支持的资料、修改与删除记录。应用不面向儿童提供独立在线注册服务。政策发生变化时会更新页面和生效日期；如需帮助，请发送邮件，避免附上完整证件、医疗或财务资料。

Privacy Policy

Effective date：2026-10-23

1. Scope and contact

This policy covers Couple for iPhone and its widgets, maintained by the Couple maintainer. The app has no separate registration system, developer-operated data backend, advertising, cross-app tracking, or third-party analytics SDK. Contact zipmaster_support@126.com for privacy and support questions.

Depending on the features you use, app data is stored on your device, in Apple CloudKit, and in iCloud Drive. The public policy and support pages are hosted by GitHub Pages, which processes webpage requests under its own policies.

2. Data and purposes

Asset balances, ledger entries, budgets, settlements, goals, wishes, anniversaries, tasks, moods, notes, and interaction records you enter support display, summaries, reminders, shared management, and exports. Names and avatars identify household members.

Document data may include names, the last four digits of numbers, expiry dates, storage locations, notes, scans, PDFs, and OCR text. Family records may include insurance and medical information, blood type, allergies, medication, and emergency contacts’ names, relationships, and phone numbers. Only add information you are entitled to store and share.

Optional location sharing processes coordinates, accuracy, and timestamps to show your recent location to household members. It is off by default and can be disabled at any time. Disabling it attempts to remove your last shared location; retry if the network request fails.

Automatic personal iCloud backup is on by default and can be disabled in We → iCloud Data Persistence. Backups include business records, backup time, summaries, and device name, but not shared location records or profile avatars. Information needed to restore the current sharing session is stored on the device and is also saved to the Apple Account’s private CloudKit and iCloud Drive when available. Widgets use a local App Group snapshot and do not connect directly to CloudKit.

3. iCloud and household sharing

Personal backups are stored in the current Apple Account’s private CloudKit space and iCloud Drive. A household synchronizes business records, attachments, avatars, and optional location through CloudKit sharing. Shared members can read and edit these records.

The owner shares a household QR image, which the other person scans to join through Apple CloudKit sharing. The QR contains one-time sharing authorization associated with the recipient’s Apple Account after binding. Share the image only with the intended member. New app versions do not write this authorization to the public CloudKit database. Information needed to restore the current sharing session is stored on the device and is also saved to the Apple Account’s private CloudKit and iCloud Drive when available.

Households created by earlier app versions may retain snapshots, member records, and binding mappings in the public CloudKit database. The owner’s updated app attempts to migrate to the current sharing model and remove old mappings. Deletion also attempts to remove legacy records and reports incomplete deletion if permissions prevent it.

4. System permissions

The camera is used for document and household QR scanning; OCR and QR recognition run on the device. The system photo picker provides only the images you select. For QR recognition, the app does not access other photos or save the selected QR image. Avatar images are stored for the profile feature and may be synchronized or backed up as described above.

Face ID and device-passcode authentication are performed by iOS; the app does not receive biometric templates or the passcode. Notifications support anniversaries, documents, tasks, budgets, and partner updates. Location permission is requested after you enable sharing. Background location updates your recent location while the app is not foregrounded, subject to iOS, power, and network limits. It is not guaranteed to be real time or suitable for emergency rescue.

Manage permissions in the app and iOS Settings. Declining optional permissions does not prevent unrelated local record features.

5. Protection and sharing boundaries

Apple protects CloudKit and iCloud data under its service and security mechanisms. The app does not claim end-to-end encryption for all records or that no third party could ever access any data. Authorized household members can access shared records.

A full document number entered in its dedicated field is encrypted on-device using AES-GCM with a key in this device’s Keychain; the key is not synchronized with app backups. The encrypted field may accompany synchronized records or backups. Scans, PDFs, OCR text, and notes can still contain full numbers or other sensitive information readable by shared members. Review them before uploading or sharing.

PDFs, CSVs, screenshots, clipboard contents, and files you explicitly share with another app are managed separately by you and their recipients.

6. Retention, deletion, and leaving

Go to We → Privacy & Security → Delete Data. The confirmation explains which members and records are affected. Disconnect on This Device only disconnects the device and does not delete the household.

Owner: delete the currently connected household, CloudKit shared records, attachments, avatars, legacy binding mappings, and accessible legacy records. The other member loses shared access. This does not erase local copies or personal backups separately retained by that member.

Participant: remove your shared name/avatar, recent location, moods, heartbeats, documents and attachments assigned to you, related document tasks/history, tasks assigned to you, and your pet interaction timestamps, then leave the share. Shared assets, ledger, wishes, anniversaries, trip notes, and family/health records without member ownership remain with the owner. Delete any personal information in those common records individually before leaving.

The operation also removes this Apple Account’s personal Couple CloudKit backup, iCloud Drive backup/recovery files, and this device’s business records, avatar cache, document encryption key, widget snapshot, reminders, and app temporary exports. When unbound, only local data and personal backups are handled; reconnect previously disconnected households to process them separately.

Deletion requires the current Apple Account, a network connection, and iCloud Drive. Failed cloud steps are not reported as complete. The app retains progress and pauses synchronization, backup, and automatic restoration until you retry successfully. Automatic backup remains off after completion until you explicitly enable it. Deletion does not delete your Apple Account.

When unbound, you may explicitly choose Clear This Device Only (Keep Cloud Data), without signing in to iCloud. This leaves personal cloud backups and previous households intact and pauses automatic restoration and backup until you explicitly re-enable the relevant functions.

7. Copies, choices, and policy updates

Records remain until you delete individual items or complete the deletion flow above. Deleting an individual shared item does not automatically remove older personal backups. Copies on another member’s device, your other devices, exported files, system device backups, and service records Apple retains under legal requirements may remain and must be managed separately. Avoid importing deleted information again from old backups.

We do not sell your data or use it for advertising profiles. Use the app to manage permissions, export supported records, and edit or delete data. The app offers no independent online registration service for children. We update this page and its effective date when practices change. Contact support for help, and avoid emailing full identity documents, medical records, or financial records.
