Folderify Privacy Policy & Disclaimer

Effective Date: March 1, 2026

Thank you for using Folderify (hereinafter referred to as "the App"). We attach great importance to your privacy and data security. This page explains how the App collects, uses, and protects your information.

I. Privacy Policy

1. Information Collection

The App does not actively collect any personal identifiable information. During use, the App will never require you to register, log in, upload, or synchronize any personal data. The App accesses your local folders or image files solely to perform folder icon previewing, styling, and icon customization. These operations are executed exclusively on your local device and will never be uploaded to any remote servers.

2. File and Data Processing

  • All folder styling, material rendering (solid tints, liquid gradients, frosted glass, brushed titanium), and icon customization operations are executed locally on your device;
  • The App does not inspect, read, modify, or transmit the contents of files inside your folders; it only accesses the folder's visual icon representation;
  • Temporary rendering caches and preview files are strictly kept within the local temporary sandbox container and are purged automatically.

3. Logs and Diagnostic Information

Unless you proactively choose to "Send Feedback" or "Report an Issue", the App does not collect any diagnostic logs or usage reports. If you reach out to us, we may collect minimal necessary information (such as macOS version, App build number) to assist in resolving the issue.

4. Third-Party Services

The App does not embed any third-party analytics, advertising, tracking, or cloud storage SDKs. All design templates, symbol rendering, and styling algorithms function completely locally on your device.

5. Data Security

The App strictly complies with the macOS App Sandbox security model, accessing folders and files only within the scope explicitly authorized by the user (such as drag-and-drop or system file picker selections). The App never scans or alters unauthorized directories.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 years of age and does not collect any information from children.

7. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will supersede earlier versions and be published within the App and on this webpage.

1. Scope of Use

The App is intended solely for lawful folder icon styling, customization, and desktop organization in personal or commercial environments. Users must not use the App for any unlawful activities.

2. Data Risks & Folder Integrity

Folderify alters only the visual icon representation of folders and does not modify, delete, or re-encode the actual content files inside your folders. However, please ensure you maintain regular backups of important data. The developer assumes no responsibility for data loss or file damage caused by hardware malfunctions, operating system faults, or user mistakes.

3. Compatibility and Performance

The App has been tested on officially supported macOS versions. However, due to macOS Finder icon caching mechanisms or third-party Finder extension behaviors, icon updates may occasionally require a Finder reload or cache refresh. We assume no liability for system-level Finder rendering delays or minor visual discrepancies across different macOS releases.

SF Symbols are the intellectual property of Apple Inc. and are governed by Apple's license agreement. Users are responsible for ensuring that custom images, stickers, or logos imported into the App do not infringe on third-party intellectual property rights.

5. Icon Reset Functionality

The App provides options to restore default system folder icons. In rare circumstances where macOS Finder cache delays icon restoration, users can also reset folder icons via the native macOS "Get Info" inspector.

We may release updates periodically to enhance rendering quality, introduce new customization features, or resolve compatibility issues. Users are responsible for keeping the App updated to ensure an optimal experience.
